Every step on the board, until it clicks.

I have taught calculus, differential equations, precalculus, algebra, trigonometry, and actuarial mathematics as an instructor of record, recitation leader, lab assistant, and grader. In every role I aim for clear reasoning, plenty of worked examples, and a room where students are comfortable asking the next question.

This fall I lead Calculus III recitations at Iowa State University. We work through vectors, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, and vector calculus by solving problems together.
