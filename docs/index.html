Mohammad Rafiqul Islam

Postdoctoral Research Associate · Iowa State University

I design and analyze Langevin algorithms: Markov chain Monte Carlo methods for sampling high-dimensional distributions across networks of agents, inside constrained domains, and at higher orders of accuracy. At Iowa State I work with David P. Herzog. I earned my Ph.D. at Florida State University in 2026, advised by Lingjiong Zhu.

Research Spotlight

All research

I.current · 2026

Constrained dynamics with singular repulsion

Collision-free stochastic particle systems inside a constrained domain, and numerical schemes that stay stable near the singularity without creating artificial collisions. Joint work with D. P. Herzog.

Project overview
II.2024 – 2026

Decentralized Bayesian sampling

Agents on a network sample a shared posterior without pooling their data. This line of work removes network bias with EXTRA-type corrections and handles convex constraints through proximal regularization.

Generalized EXTRA SGLDDecentralized Proximal SGLD
III.2025

High-order Langevin Monte Carlo

Samplers built from \(P\)-th order Langevin dynamics through splitting and accurate integration. Their Wasserstein guarantees depend better on the dimension and the accuracy level as the order grows.
